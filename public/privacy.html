Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MannieNG ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information about you when you use our website and services at manni.onrender.com. By using MannieNG, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use MannieNG, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your username, email address, and Nigerian phone number.
  • Password: stored in encrypted (hashed) form. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Earnings data: your balance, completed tasks, transfer history, and referral activity.
  • Bank details: the bank name, account number, and account name you provide for transfers.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited on our site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your MannieNG account.
  • Track tasks you complete and credit your balance.
  • Process transfer requests to your Nigerian bank account.
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud or abuse of the platform.
  • Send service-related messages (e.g., transaction confirmations, account updates).
  • Improve the platform, fix bugs, and add new features.
  • Comply with legal obligations under Nigerian law.

3. Third-Party Services

MannieNG works with third-party providers to offer you surveys and advertising. These providers may receive anonymized or pseudonymized data to deliver their services:

  • CPX Research — survey provider. When you use the Surveys page, CPX receives your user ID, username, and email to match you with surveys and pay us for completions.
  • Adsterra — advertising network. Displays ads on our site and may use cookies or device identifiers for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Google AdSense — advertising network (where applicable). Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Render — hosting provider. Hosts our servers and processes all traffic to our site.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

MannieNG uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. This is not a tracking cookie — it simply holds a small amount of data so you don't have to log in every time you visit.

Third-party advertisers (such as Adsterra and Google AdSense) may set their own cookies to show you relevant ads. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you want your account deleted, email us at support@mannieng.com and we will remove your personal information within 30 days.

Note: some records (like transfer history) may need to be retained longer for accounting and legal compliance.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information:

  • Passwords are hashed before storage (we use bcrypt).
  • All traffic to and from MannieNG is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Access to user data is limited to the site owner and trusted staff.

No system is 100% secure. If you suspect your account has been compromised, change your password immediately and email us.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@mannieng.com.

8. Children's Privacy

MannieNG is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that a user is under 18, we will delete their account and any associated data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of MannieNG after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us:

  • Email: support@mannieng.com
  • Website: manni.onrender.com
